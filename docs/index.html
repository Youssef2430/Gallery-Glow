Gallery Glow — Support

Gallery Glow turns your Apple TV into a living art gallery: full-screen classic paintings from Hopper, Monet, Botticelli, Church, and more, plus soothing animated gradients and Top Shelf integration.

Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock everything?

Gallery Glow Lifetime is a one-time purchase that unlocks every painting, animated gradient, and Top Shelf deep link — including all future gallery additions. There is no subscription.

I purchased on another Apple TV. How do I restore?

Open the paywall from the banner on the home screen and select Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to purchase. Family Sharing is supported.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
