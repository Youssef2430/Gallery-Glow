What this figure models

This is a mechanical explanation of the source code. Controls simulate selection, access, daily rollover, and recent history locally. They do not make purchases or run the native app. Colored screen content is artwork or a palette preview; the gradient preview is not a reproduction of SwiftUI’s mesh animation.

The 76 physical parts are illustrative. Catalog counts and limits are extracted from the Swift files at build time. Cache sizes are configured budgets, not live usage. Top Shelf also has featured and Director’s Cut sections beyond the eight recent mounts shown here.
