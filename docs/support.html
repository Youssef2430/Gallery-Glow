Gallery Glow · Apple TV

Support

Gallery Glow turns your Apple TV into a living art gallery: full-screen classic paintings from Hopper, Monet, Botticelli, Church, and more, plus soothing animated gradients and Top Shelf integration.

What can I enjoy for free?

Browse the entire collection and enjoy the Painting of the Day in full screen for free, including from Top Shelf. The free artwork changes each day. If a painting is already playing at midnight, it keeps playing until you leave; reopening it follows the new day's selection.

How do I unlock everything?

Gallery Glow Lifetime is a one-time purchase that unlocks every painting on any day and every animated gradient, including all future gallery additions. There is no subscription.

I purchased on another Apple TV. How do I restore?

Select Explore lifetime on the home screen, then select Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to purchase.

What if the price does not load?

Select Try loading price again after checking your connection. You can still restore an existing purchase or choose Keep browsing.

My purchase is awaiting approval

You can keep browsing while Apple processes approval. Your lifetime access updates automatically when the purchase is confirmed.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
