Gallery Glow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Gallery Glow is an Apple TV app that displays classic paintings and ambient gradients. Your privacy is simple to explain: Gallery Glow does not collect any data.

Data collection

Gallery Glow does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. All artwork is bundled with the app and displayed entirely on your device.

Purchases

The optional lifetime unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment information. Purchase state is recorded by the App Store on your Apple Account and restored through Apple's standard mechanisms.

Local preferences

The app stores small preferences (such as recently viewed paintings) locally on your Apple TV using Apple's standard user defaults. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Children's privacy

Gallery Glow does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a revised date.
